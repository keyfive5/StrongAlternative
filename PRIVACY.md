# Overload — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

Overload does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **No account.** There is no sign-up and no login.
- **No network.** The app makes no network requests. There is no server, no
  sync, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK that sends data
  anywhere.
- **Your data stays on your phone.** Workouts, routines, body measurements and
  settings are stored in a file inside the app's own private storage on your
  device. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **Import and export happen only when you ask.** If you import a CSV or
  backup file, the app reads the one file you pick. If you export, the file is
  handed to the iOS share sheet and goes only where you send it.
- **Health data.** Overload does not read from or write to Apple Health.
